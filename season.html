Head-to-Head vs the Field

Two ways to read it. Top boats: when both clubs raced the same event, did our best boat finish ahead of their best boat — that's the "who won the race between us" record. All boats: counting every OARS boat against every one of their boats in the same event, the share of finishes where we were ahead — that's depth. Season to date, all races.

Rowers

Every rower's record, built automatically from the lineups and results — no extra entry needed. Open a name for their race-by-race line.

Coxswains

Coxing record per athlete. Boat penalties counts steering calls charged to their boat (buoy, bow number, order, late to start) — the closest thing a head race gives to a cox score.

Combinations

Every crew that has gone out together, grouped by boat class. Off pace is the gap to that event's winner, which makes different days and conditions roughly comparable. Read swap results as direction, not proof — a one-outing difference can be the water, the draw, or the day.
