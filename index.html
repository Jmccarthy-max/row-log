Practice Day Fuel ⏱️

Coach Jenn
Food is absolute energy. Practices are challenging enough — don't run on an empty tank and expect your body to go far or do great things. Recovery is hands down the most important part of training.
🥪 Mini meal · 1½–2½ hrs before practice or a race
  • ½ turkey sandwich; 1 orange
  • Protein yogurt; ¼ cup granola
  • Rice and any lean meat (ground turkey, rotisserie chicken, tuna)
  • ¾ cup cereal; 6 oz milk
  • Protein/granola bar; banana
  • Natural nut butter (max 1 Tbsp — high fat slows digestion and upsets stomachs) and jelly sandwich; ¼ cup or 2 handfuls of grapes
  • Dave's Killer bagel, thin layer of cream cheese
🍌 Carbs · within 20 min before
  • 1 banana
  • 2 handfuls of dried fruit or fruit chews
  • 20 oz sport drink (Body Armor, Prime, Gatorade, coconut water)
  • 1 orange / fruit / fruit pouch
  • 2 rice cakes
  • 1 oz salted pretzels
🔋 Recovery snack · within 30 min of finishing
In the car or at the boathouse — then a normal well-balanced dinner later.
  • 12 oz chocolate milk
  • Applesauce pouch; protein shake
  • ¾ cup cereal; 8 oz milk; 1 Tbsp raisins
  • 10+ g protein yogurt (Greek); ¼ cup blueberries; 12 oz sport drink
  • RX bar
  • ½ turkey sandwich; 1 banana

Race Week Fuel 🏁

Coach Jenn
Training fatigue is cumulative — skip meals and by Thursday you're glycogen-depleted, and hard pieces on empty are "NOT a good place to be." Plus: a gallon of water daily.
🛒 Stock the kitchen for race week
Have these around (her list): sweet potatoes · pasta · quinoa/brown rice · multigrain bread & wraps · bagels · Greek yogurt + granola · eggs, chicken, fish, lean turkey · quality lunch meat · cottage cheese · sea-salted almonds & rice cakes · natural nut butter · hummus & pretzels · bananas · raisins & fruit snacks · veggies · sports drinks.
📅 Mon–Thu · top up the tank
  • Breakfast: protein shake (protein + banana + oats + nut butter) or Greek yogurt + granola + berries
  • Lunch: turkey or chicken + whole-grain wrap + sweet potato + greens
  • Snacks: yogurt & granola · hard-boiled eggs + apple · watermelon · half sandwich
  • At practice: sports drink + water
  • Post practice: chocolate milk, or protein shake + banana (carb + protein, fast)
  • Dinner: fish, chicken or steak + whole-wheat pasta/quinoa/sweet potato + salad or veggies
  • Before bed: cottage cheese or nut butter — slow protein that repairs muscles overnight
🏁 Friday · pre-race day
  • Breakfast: egg muffins + multigrain toast with peanut butter
  • Snacks: yogurt + granola (low-GI carb matters here) · turkey/tuna + sweet potato
  • Lunch: chicken salad + whole-grain wrap + fruit
  • Dinner out: balanced plate — bigger portion of lower-GI carbs, medium protein, small fat
  • Then: "Drink water and GO TO BED."
⏱️ Race day · timing is everything
  • Breakfast: balanced, lower-GI carbs — go easy on protein & fat until after racing
  • 1–2 hrs before: mid/low-GI carbs only
  • 20 min–1 hr before: high-GI carbs — watermelon, grapes, raisins, honey, banana, sport drink or chews
  • Right after racing: high-GI carbs again + a little protein
  • 2 hrs post: balanced meal
  • Post-race meal: heavier protein for recovery and muscle rebuilding — plus extra fluids and your electrolyte drink
🍚 High-GI vs low-GI carbs
High-GI (right before / right after racing): sports drinks, banana, raisins, watermelon, pretzels, white bread/bagel/potato/rice, fruit snacks.

Low/mid-GI (meals 2+ hrs from racing, with protein & fat): pasta, multigrain bread/wraps, beans, brown rice, apples, pears, sweet potatoes.

Hydration

0 oz

Coach Jenn says 💧

Not enough fluid is the #1 thing rowers fall short on — exercise dulls your thirst, so don't wait until you feel it.
  • Before practice: ½–1 cup every 20 minutes (about 20 oz per hour) from the time you wake up.
  • At practice: an electrolyte drink (like Body Armor) for the full 2-hour session — sip 30–60g of carbs per hour, don't chug.
  • Training days: an extra bottle before practice, 1–2 during, 1+ after. On non-training days, half your body weight in mL of water.
  • Sport drink: best at the halfway mark of practice, or when you switch from lift to erg/row. It needs 15–20g of sugar per serving — Body Armor, coconut water, Gatorade, Liquid IV, or a DIY mix.
  • Why it matters: dehydration raises your heart rate, makes the work feel harder, and invites heat illness.
